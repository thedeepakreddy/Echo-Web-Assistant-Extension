Lisbon picked to host the 2031 Ocean Summit

The United Nations announced on Tuesday that Lisbon will host the 2031 Ocean Summit, beating bids from Cape Town and Sydney.

Organisers expect about 12,000 delegates over five days, and the city plans to open a new ferry line to the venue at Parque das Nações.

Portugal's environment minister said the summit would focus on deep-sea mining rules and coral reef restoration.

The previous summit, held in Nairobi in 2027, drew roughly 9,500 delegates.

Tickets for public sessions go on sale in March 2030.
